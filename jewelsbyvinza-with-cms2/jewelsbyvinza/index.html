Frequently Asked Questions

Is cash on delivery (COD) available across Pakistan?

Yes, Jewel's by Vinza offers cash on delivery across Pakistan, including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and other cities. You pay when your order arrives.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, fill in your name, phone number and delivery address, and your order is sent directly to our WhatsApp (0329-1872757) for confirmation — no account or sign-up needed.

Is the jewelry real gold?

Our pieces are gold plated and silver plated fashion jewelry — designed to look premium and last with proper care, at an affordable price point rather than solid gold pricing.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timelines are confirmed on WhatsApp after you place your order, and typically range from a few days depending on your city.

Do you sell watches, rings, bracelets and press-on nails too?

Yes — alongside necklaces and pendants, we stock gold and silver plated watches, rings, bracelets and bangles, and press-on/false nail sets. Browse each category from the menu above.
